## 1. Purpose and justification

Onboarding in many organisations is handled through scattered emails, paper forms and ad hoc check-ins. New hires wait for access, HR repeats the same explanations, and managers cannot see who is stuck. The EOP is a fully digital, web-based platform that moves the whole onboarding journey into one place: documents, tasks, training and progress tracking.

## 2. Objectives

1. Give every new hire a single portal showing what they must do before and after day one.
2. Let HR create, assign and track onboarding tasks without chasing people by email.
3. Replace paper and email document collection with secure digital submission.
4. Give managers and HR a live view of onboarding progress per employee.

## 3. Measurable success criteria

Targets below are planning assumptions set at charter approval. They are the baseline that the closure report measures against.

- 100% of onboarding tasks for a new hire visible in one dashboard
- Document submission completed in the portal with no paper or email step
- Reduction in HR time spent on onboarding follow-ups (baseline to be measured at pilot)
- UAT passed with all must-have user stories accepted by the product owner

## 4. High-level scope

**In scope:** user login with role-based access (new hire, HR admin, line manager), onboarding checklist and task assignment, document upload and verification, welcome and policy content, progress dashboard, notifications, admin reporting.

**Out of scope (v1):** payroll integration, third-party HRIS integration, native mobile apps, multi-language support, advanced analytics.
