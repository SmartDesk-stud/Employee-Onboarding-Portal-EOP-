# Business Case and Stakeholder Register: EOP

## 1. Business case

**Problem.** Onboarding relies on emails, paper forms and manual follow-ups. New hires do not know what is expected of them, HR repeats the same steps for every hire, and managers have no live view of progress.

**Options considered**

| Option | Summary | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Do nothing | Keep email and paper | Rejected: problem persists |
| Buy an off-the-shelf HR suite | Full-featured, recurring licence cost | Rejected: cost and unused features |
| Build a focused web portal (EOP) | Covers onboarding only, built by the team | Selected |

**Expected benefits:** one place for tasks, documents and content; less HR admin; visible progress; consistent experience for every hire.

**Costs:** team time only. Tooling limited to free tiers (Jira, Google Workspace, Google Stitch, free hosting) [TEAM TO CONFIRM actual tools used].
