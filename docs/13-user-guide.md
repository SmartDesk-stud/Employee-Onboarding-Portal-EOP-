# User Guide: Employee Onboarding Portal

## For new hires

1. **Log in** with the details sent by HR.
2. **Open your checklist.** Your dashboard lists every task with a due date.
3. **Complete tasks** by following the instructions and ticking each one when done.
4. **Upload documents** under the Documents tab. Each file shows a status: pending, approved or rejected. If rejected, read the reason and upload a corrected file.
5. **Check your progress** bar to see how much is left.
6. **Read the welcome and policy pages** for company information.

## For HR administrators

1. **Create a checklist** from a template or add tasks one by one with title, description and due date.
2. **Assign it** to a new hire.
3. **Review documents** and approve or reject. A reason is required when rejecting.
4. **Monitor the dashboard** for progress and overdue tasks.
5. **Run reports** for completion rates.

## For managers

1. Open the dashboard to see your team members' onboarding progress.
2. Follow up on overdue tasks directly with the new hire or HR.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Cannot log in | Check your email and password, then ask HR to reset |
| Upload fails | Check the file type and size, then retry |
| Task not showing | Confirm with HR that it was assigned |

## Getting help

Contact your HR administrator through the support channel your organisation sets up for the portal.
